Level 3a: components, the Holocene Auto app

The app is a thin shell: it decides whether the pad may open (parked only), hosts it in a WebView and records audio natively because WebView getUserMedia is unreliable on the head unit. Recording is parked-only by construction.

Level 3b: components, the Hermes gateway plugin

Two JetStream consumers, one handler. The interactive lane is the voice lane: 2 slots, and since today a wall clock on every turn. The batch lane keeps its 4 slots and runs long grooming turns unbounded unless configured.
