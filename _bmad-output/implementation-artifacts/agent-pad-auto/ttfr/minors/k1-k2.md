Date: 2026-10-21. Simulator `emulator-5554` only (no physical device, no Mac). `apps/auto/app/build.gradle.kts` is
untouched: `versionCode 12`, `0.2.10-internal`. v12 releases and the Mac copy were not touched. Debug build installed with
`mise run auto:dev`. Code: `SpeechArbiter.kt`, `SpeechHost.kt`, `DriveFeedback.kt`, `MainActivity.kt`,
`SpeechArbiterTest.kt`.

## K1: the heard-echo retry after a focus loss

**Before.** A heard echo cut by a focus loss (or refused the focus) was asked for again every 2 s for 10 s, whatever took
the focus. A permanent loss (another app took it for good) was retried as often as a call. A retry took the focus with a
new request, which a holder that is not a call or an exclusive request does not refuse: it could talk over what was still
holding the focus.

**Now.**
- `focusLost(id, reason, transient)`: only `AUDIOFOCUS_LOSS_TRANSIENT` (a call, the car's assistant) keeps the echo. A
  permanent `AUDIOFOCUS_LOSS` drops it at once (`drop ... reason=focus_taken_for_good`), with no further request.
- After a transient loss the app does **not** poll. `DriveFeedback` keeps the loss's focus request registered and the
  system reports `AUDIOFOCUS_GAIN` when the holder is gone (`onFocusBack(true)`, `SpeechArbiter.focusReturned`). Only then is
  the echo asked for again, so it cannot take the focus from something that still has it. A GAIN is still limited by the
  10 s window (`FOCUS_RETURN_MS`); an echo whose focus never returns expires (`expired`). A loss that turns permanent while
  waiting drops it (`onFocusBack(false)`).
- An echo refused outright (a call was on before it started) is still tried every 2 s inside the same window: a refused
  request cannot take anything from anybody, and there is no request to wait on.
- An echo is still said again at most once, never over a reply, a recording or the arming prompt (the queue's own rules:
  `pump` starts nothing while something plays or the mic is busy), and an ack or cue is never replayed.

**Tests** (`SpeechArbiterTest`, new or rewritten): permanent loss drops and never asks again (ten polls, no request);
transient loss waits and makes no request while the call holds the focus; no GAIN in the window means `expired`; a loss that
turns permanent drops it; a returned focus never barges in on a playing reply or a recording; the older call-cut tests
now use a transient loss and a GAIN. 197 JUnit tests in the app, 0 failures.

The sim cannot produce either focus kind (no second app, no call), so K1 is proven by JUnit.

## K2: the SENT tone belongs to its note

**Before.** The SENT tone went ahead of what the recording held back only if it was offered within 6 s of the mic closing
(`NOTE_ACK_WINDOW_MS`). A slow upload lost the priority, and with the ack's 6 s deadline it also expired behind a reply.

**Now.**
- `Utterance.note`: the STOP ack takes a token from `SpeechArbiter.noteStopped()`; the SENT tone takes the oldest open
  note from `noteSent()`. An ack with a note goes first among what waits (`note_ack` in the decision log), whenever it
  is offered. A "sent" with no open note (a retry of an older note, `reason=retry`) keeps the lowest rank. An unanswered
  note stops being claimable after 2 min (`NOTE_OPEN_MS`), so a note that never got an answer cannot promote a later retry.
- The priority only chooses among what waits: `pump` starts nothing while an utterance plays, so a reply that is already
  playing is never cut or passed.
- A note's SENT tone waits up to 30 s (`SENT_WAIT_MS`) instead of the ack's 6 s, so a reply that plays longer than 6 s does
  not make the driver miss that the note went. The ordinary ack's 6 s deadline is unchanged. This was found on the sim, see
  below.

**Tests**: a SENT tone 10 s after Stop still goes before the echo and clip that waited; a late SENT never cuts a playing
reply and is said right after it; one that waits past 6 s is not dropped; a retry's "sent" keeps its rank; tokens are
spent in order and expire.

## Proven on the sim

The null sink `holo_testmic` (`paplay`, never gRPC injection) fed the emulator's mic; the speaker tap and logcat ran
throughout. Driver: [`tools/drive_k.py`](tools/drive_k.py). Test memos went to holocene-pm. Clock offset -510 ms
([`clock.txt`](clock.txt)).

| run | what | result |
|---|---|---|
| `n1`, `k`, `kslow` (first K2 build, 6 s ack deadline) | a normal memo; then a memo M whose reply starts, and 1.2 s into the reply a recording R opens over it | the reply is `halt`ed (`reason=stopped`) before the arming; R's STOP ack and SENT tone (`queued ack:sent ... behind_ack,note_ack`) play first; the reply is re-offered and plays after them (`queued reply ... behind_ack`, then `start reply`). In `kslow` R's upload was held 8 s by pausing the proxy: the SENT tone arrived 8.2 s after Stop, was queued `behind_reply,note_ack` (a note ack with no 6 s window), the reply that was playing was **not** cut, and the tone then **expired** 3 ms after the reply ended because the reply played 6.4 s (the 6 s ack deadline). That led to `SENT_WAIT_MS`. |
| `kslow2` (final build, installed again with `auto:dev`) | the same, upload held 8 s | the SENT tone landed 8.2 s after Stop with the reply already over: `queued ack:sent reason=note_ack`, started 1 ms later, ended 0.6 s later. No drop. |
