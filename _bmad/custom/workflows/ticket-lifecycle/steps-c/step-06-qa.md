# Step 6: Submit QA Evidence and Follow Obligations

## Goal

Submit QA results and perform focused repair only when Lifecycle authorizes it.

## Actions

1. Evaluate every acceptance criterion and cite the evidence.
2. Submit QA evidence/review verdict through the canonical observation contract.
3. Refetch authoritative state, obligations, blockers, and frontier.
4. If repair is legal, delegate focused repair and resubmit evidence.
5. If retries are exhausted or a blocker is authoritative, report it.

Momo never decides that QA advanced state. Lifecycle reconciles the evidence.
