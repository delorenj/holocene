# Edit Step 1: Assess Client-Policy Change

## Allowed scope

Edit mode may change Momo ranking preferences, AC assessment guidance, display
labels, reporting cadence, or provider projection mappings.

It may not edit Lifecycle spec/state, transition graphs, obligations,
capability rules, command schemas, or authoritative provider mappings. Those
belong to Lifecycle/Bloodbank governance.

## Actions

1. Describe the desired client-policy change and business reason.
2. Confirm it cannot create a new legal action or state transition.
3. Identify source/generated parity copies and affected client tests.
4. Continue to step-02-apply.md only when the boundary remains intact.
