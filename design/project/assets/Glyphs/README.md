# Glyphs

The seven signal glyphs on a 10-unit grid, drawn in their light-theme inks. They are for docs, slides and notifications. In product UI, render `Glyph` or `Signal` from the bundle so the colour follows the theme.

| File | State | Light ink | Dark ink |
|---|---|---|---|
| `signal-you.svg` | Diamond, needs you | `#835d13` (ochre) | `#d8b36e` |
| `signal-broken.svg` | Square, broken | `#9c4230` (brick) | `#de8c7a` |
| `signal-stuck.svg` | Hourglass, stuck | `#67567d` (heather) | `#b3a2ca` |
| `signal-working.svg` | Ring with a turning arc, working; the arc rotates once per `duration-orbit` in the product | `#3a6479` (lake) | `#89b5cd` |
| `signal-unknown.svg` | Dotted ring, no signal | `#606761` (ink-3) | `#909590` |
| `signal-quiet.svg` | Dash, quiet | `#606761` (ink-3) | `#909590` |
| `signal-cleared.svg` | Disc, cleared | `#43673f` (moss) | `#92b98d` |
